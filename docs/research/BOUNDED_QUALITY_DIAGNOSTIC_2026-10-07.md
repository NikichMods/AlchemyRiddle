unjustified redundant ordinary clue. Diagnose full-graph compatible false chains
separately from unchanged vanilla success formulas. Do not assume every stable
chain is a valid target formula. Report clue omission witnesses only as counts.

For retained packages record constant count terms, effective slot scope,
immediate literals, constant/uniquely identifiable conditional endpoints, repeated
independent work and concrete cross-clue dependencies. These are review evidence,
not calibrated difficulty or enjoyment scores.

Keep unrefuted, certified and full-model candidates distinct. A public-policy
route may use visible tags and earned outcomes, never the hidden answer to choose
its next query. Test an unresolved edge of a live tag-compatible candidate;
prefer a balanced split of current hypotheses, then higher certification support,
with seeded ties. Replay actual hidden outcomes only after each selection.

whose complete-model validity was checked independently. A lucky full submission

routes; these limited policies do not prove robustness for every plausible player.

target-irrelevant queries and remaining hypotheses. For each actual route show
