# Campaign ladders and naming — 2026-10-10

Status: accepted campaign principles below; numbered curriculum and naming
examples are reviewable proposals, not calibrated difficulty or playable content.
Research only; production remains BLOCKED. Companion:
`CAMPAIGN_SIMULATION_PROPOSAL_2026-10-10.md`.

## Accepted clarifications

- Show difficulty in player and developer modes, without a player-facing
  “provisional” prefix. Internally record that it is an intended band, not a
  measured human difficulty. Also show arity; developers additionally see rung,
  focus and starting-state provenance.
- Carry both stable and incompatible observations immediately. A solved known
  three-slot formula establishes its two adjacent stable relations; a failed
  mixture alone establishes neither relation's polarity. Generator considers
  earned knowledge. Reagent identities, properties and relations are stable
  across the campaign; local card positions are not durable identities.
- Eight optional decorative colour variants are two-slot side investigations,
  not eight extra fixed late rungs. White/black paint are outside this group.
  Surface optional work from a visible decorative need; generate at the current
  two-slot experience/knowledge state and freeze once started. User explicitly
  accepts counting a completed optional investigation as ordinary two-slot
  practice. Optional work is never necessary to reach mature/MAX difficulty.
- Provide Science refill in every new campaign stand mode, including guests and
  online players. Keep it subordinate to synthesis. Record refills separately
  from actual paid-action spending. This does not add refill mechanics to vanilla
  Graveyard Keeper or retroactively change historical finite-budget trials.
- Use interesting varied dark-fantasy names with restrained magical science,
  dark humour and self-irony. Vary phrase structures, not just words in one
  template. Target names must not reveal answers. Visible reagent properties
  should normally have a credible relationship to reagent names; occasional
  deliberately contrary cosmetic humour is welcome, not the dominant style.

## How to read the ladders

The 16/19 positions are coverage for core formula variants, not fixed assignments
of named vanilla products. A rung means the next completed investigation of that
arity in the core reference scenario. Target demand can interleave arities and
optional practice can advance the same experience; do not mistake these reference
positions for a mandatory 35-stop quest. Alternative formulas are independent
investigations. A developer jump needs an explicit saved/seeded starting state.

The tables propose the requested approximate campaign. Bands serve as player
labels. Focus is an authoring aim: use a valid suitable package, never force a
particular operator or reduce quality to fill a cell. MAX means the mature ceiling
is available; Boss denotes a selected peak with deeper interaction. Neither
means “answer placed last” or “more paid clicks”. Early onboarding is compact;
familiar exact count/direction changes need no redundant standalone lesson.

These labels are proposed targets, not evidence that a generated package meets
them. If knowledge makes a particular investigation easier, retain the facts and
record that outcome. Show the assigned label consistently; never change the
puzzle after starting to restore the planned difficulty. Human play and feasible
fields may change this schedule before campaign content is frozen.

## Illustrative interleaving and optional work

One capability/demand scenario starts with two-slot positions 1–3, then alternates
two-slot position 4 / three-slot positions 1–2, two-slot positions 5–6 /
three-slot positions 3–4, then two-slot position 7 / three-slot positions 5–7.
Continue with whichever arity the next visible target needs. This is a simulator
scenario, not a claim about vanilla story timing or mandatory workstation order.

Example: a player has completed three two-slot investigations and chooses a
decorative target. It becomes the next two-slot practice investigation at their
current state. Three-slot experience does not advance. A different player who
postpones decoration until mature play gets a mature puzzle for that same target.
Once started, its state is fixed. No precreated journal entry forces either player
to do it, and no extra eight-step “paint endgame” follows the ladders.

The full core reference run, shorter demand scenario and optional-detour scenario
need separate replay identities. A short scenario need not visit all mature rows;
its purpose is to check that it can still encounter the ceiling.

## Portable naming design

This is for original synthetic stand content and, where appropriate, investigation
flavour. Vanilla reagent/product names remain canonical in the future mod. No
runtime text model, network service or automatic renaming of vanilla items is
implied. The stand's invented titles are not invented evidence about the game.

Separate two inputs:

1. **Target title:** select from an independent curated title bank using campaign
   seed and target identity. Do not pass hidden answer tags, ingredient identities,
   relation outcomes or clue packages into the naming choice. An atmospheric title
   must not become a second coded condition. Optional paint needs may have a visible
   colour as their ordinary goal; that does not reveal ingredient composition.
2. **Reagent name:** select an authored semantic archetype compatible with its
   visible tag signature and role. Each archetype contains several complete,
   grammatically checked names of different constructions. Assign once to the
   global reagent identity and persist it in the campaign manifest.

For example, an archetype authored for aquatic + animal + dark essence can offer
“Чернильный дух”, “Эхо кракена” and “Последний вздох глубины”. A script does not
discover the kraken association: we supply it once in the catalogue, and the
script selects an eligible authored variant. These names are illustrative, not
a new taxonomic rule; the visible tags remain the authoritative puzzle facts.

Start with small curated records rather than a general Russian morphology engine:
`archetypeId`, `role`, `requiredTags`, `allowedTags`, `variants` (stable IDs and
complete text), and a separately marked `ironicVariants` list. The required and
allowed sets let us reject semantically unsupported combinations. Exact tag
coverage is authored against the actual synthetic world before play; if a
signature has no suitable record, author it rather than fabricate a fallback.

Use a separate naming random stream and stable IDs. Name assignment cannot move
the answer or alter generator sampling. Persist assignments instead of regenerating
on navigation/reordering. Avoid duplicate display names in one campaign; if the
bank is exhausted, enlarge it before release. No automatic clue evaluation,
hidden “name tags” or additional properties inferred from poetry.

### Small editorial sample: genuinely different constructions

These are candidate names, not a frozen world or a recipe list. Tag associations
are illustrative visible properties, not validation of a complete naming bank.

| Role / visible association | Normal name examples | Construction variety |
|---|---|---|
| Powder / mineral | «Кварцевая мука», «Прах колокола», «Соль без моря» | adjective+noun; genitive; short phrase |
| Powder / plant + dark | «Труха», «Чёрный папоротник, тонкий помол», «Пепел ведьминого сада» | one word; preparation label; provenance |
| Fluid / aquatic | «Подлёдная вода», «Отлив во флаконе», «Рассол русалки» | direct name; image; source |
| Fluid / plant + slime | «Сок сонной росянки», «Зелёная тягучесть», «Болотная патока» | source; substance quality; compact noun phrase |
| Essence / aquatic + animal + dark | «Чернильный дух», «Эхо кракена», «Последний вздох глубины» | compact; mythical source; evocative phrase |
| Essence / animal + slime | «Липкая душа», «Слизневый след», «Посмертная вытяжка улитки» | quality; trace; laboratory preparation |

Rare deliberately contrary variants can include «Нежность» for a grotesque slime
essence or «Бархатная забота» for an unpleasant animal preparation. Their humour
is cosmetic: do not give a visibly dark substance a false “plant” tag to justify
the joke. At first use a few explicitly authored exceptions in the frozen world,
not another adaptive rarity/scoring system. Exact frequency remains editorial
tuning and must not dominate ordinary semantic names.

Independent target title sample: «Эликсир отложенного проклятия», «Тихая поправка
к мирозданию», «Малая милость», «Настойка здравого сомнения», «Опыт № 13:
почти благополучный», «Бальзам для трудных понедельников». Mix scientific,
fairy-tale, compact and quietly comic constructions; do not make every title a
punchline. These examples do not refer to any hidden formula.

## Next concrete implementation boundary

Build a consistent versioned synthetic world/manifest next: persistent reagent
identities, tags, curated names, relation graph, target IDs/titles and curriculum
profiles. Check coverage and that actual earned positive/negative knowledge is
supported by generation/route estimation. Only then populate playable levels
and integrate profiles/UI. The tables and samples alone are not 35 new playable
puzzles, and they do not authorize replacing the running friends' trial.
