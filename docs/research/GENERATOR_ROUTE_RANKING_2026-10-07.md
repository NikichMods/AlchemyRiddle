## Predeclared experiment

User-authorized generator-selection comparison on current settings. Existing
criteria remain; only a soft mean-new-pair-check term differs between arms.
Research-only, no production generator or new runtime/UI behavior.

Scope: all 19 ordinary three-slot variants from the verified private core,
8 seeded 3x3x3 fields per variant, at most 512 sampled 2/3-clue packages per
field (77,824 attempts). Full ingredient availability; tutorials and two-slot
generation excluded. This tests the existing three-slot research-generation
envelope, not all field sizes or save/progression readiness.

Reuse reasoning_diversity_screen's true weak clues and option_from_combo gates:
public clue necessity, 4–12 live triples, 2–4 branches in at least one adjacent
orientation, bounded author-level elimination witness, target present. Require
unique complete-model answer and full-model clue necessity in both arms. Reject
flat/repeated control packages and immediately handed implication antecedents
under the existing ordinary/mature rubric. These implemented structural gates
are proxies; manual review still owns human interest/depth, not a new oracle.

Keep at most 12 candidates per target by deterministic seeded reservoir sampling
after gates, independent of route cost. Both arms use the exact same frozen pool.
At each target compare no initial pair knowledge, one and four already-known
stable relations sampled from the full fixed graph before fields/packages;
do not deliberately provide an answer edge. Only relations in a selected field
are surfaced, without discarding personally known knowledge. Negative knowledge
across investigations remains outside this experiment's accepted scope.

Mean-route estimator: 16 fixed seeds per each of the two existing public policies,
equal policy weighting (32 replays per candidate/knowledge state). Stop on chemical
certification or a unique public survivor. Hidden outcomes used after query choice.
This is a sampled mechanical proxy, not measured human average. Cap at 21,888
replays (19*12*3*32), 6 minutes computation; on cap report partial work.

Baseline selection: retain structural gates above and reuse the existing
anti-clump sequence's numeric diversity/recency/wave score. The sequence's previous
selected packages differ by arm normally; same target order/candidate pool/seeds.
Two desired pace profiles, 3 and 5, are intended settings, not calibrated difficulty
labels. Compare baseline with additive penalty strengths 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2.
Penalty at mean m and preferred count c:
`max(0,m-c)^2 + max(0,m-5)^2 + 2*max(0,m-7)^2 + max(0,2-m)^2`.
Two-check routes are not penalized; no hard rejection or paid-action minimum.
The final short-route term is zero when relevant earned stable knowledge is
present, so the comparison cannot penalize an earned zero/one-check shortcut.
This formula/weights are experimental, not accepted production tuning.

Three fixed target-order seeds per profile/context; report mean route estimate,
selected fractions in 2–5 / above 5 / above 7, route tail, old-score/diversity
trade-offs and candidate coverage. Baseline retention, missing targets and floor
failures stay visible; do not repair by changing target, tags or weakening gates.
Review at most six changed selections, recording only abstract structural measures
publicly; exact certificates and identities remain private outside Git.

The primary result supports the user's additive framing. Existing branch and
reasoning gates already remove the isolated-nine-triple failure mode; the baseline
is near the desired interval, without any selected mean above seven. A weak new
term improves lower/middle pacing without relaxing gates. It is not a repair of
an established broken generator. Preferred=5 needs little adjustment because
baseline already averages about four; the term does not manufacture extra checks.

With one/four globally known stable relations, preferred=3, strength 0.25:
held-out means change 4.009→3.552 and 3.519→3.227. Many sampled global relations
are irrelevant to a given field; these are declared contexts, not a general
save-state or density simulation. Earned shortcuts remain permitted.

All strengths reduce the overall mean in the fresh preferred=3 comparison:
0.25/0.5/1/2 give 3.622/3.570/3.517/3.448. Stronger adjustment increases adjacent
family overlap from baseline 0.130 to as much as 0.320 and old diversity cost from
1.294 to 2.130. Individual changes are not guaranteed monotonic: selection alters
later recency penalties. Among six reviewed changes at strength 0.5, four shorten
the sampled mean and two increase it (one from 3.344 to 5.844); all keep formal
and ordinary structural gates. Thus this term must remain one weighted preference,
not a replacement for other axes or a promise that every selected puzzle shortens.

Structural review of the six examples finds no newly handed antecedent or flat
control package. Cross-slot dependencies remain present. This establishes retention
of the measured structural envelope, **not measured player enjoyment**.

At the provisional 2/5 prices, fresh selected packages have pair-research average
cost below uniformly ordered direct submissions in both arms. One four-known,
preferred=5 weak-penalty sequence has a tied-cost candidate (1/54 selections).
Economic attractiveness remains a separate check; the proxy is not a proof of
production Science balance, particularly when real prior beliefs are nonuniform.

### Coverage and boundaries

One of 19 targets has no surviving candidate in the bounded sample in either arm.
This is a baseline sampling/structural coverage gap, not a new penalty rejection
and not proof that the target cannot support a puzzle. No formula identity is
published, no target substituted, no gate weakened. Full ingredient availability,
3x3x3 fields, 2/3-clue grammar, positive-only stored knowledge and three sequence
orders bound these conclusions. Intellectual-interest scoring remains partly
manual; two public policies are not a calibrated human solver.

Judgment: retain mean new-check cost as an additive soft ranking criterion.
Prefer a weak starting coefficient in further research; do not adopt the strongest
penalty solely to minimize clicks. No exact production coefficient, calibrated
band assignment or new rejection threshold is accepted here. The next bounded
question is the single missing target's candidate coverage under existing gates,
not another investigation of the previously rejected long-route fixture.
