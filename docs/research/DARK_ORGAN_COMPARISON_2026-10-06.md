## Compared models

A — accepted working model:
- current eight source/property tags;
- plus **Dark** on the nine Health / Death / Acceleration Powder/Fluid/Essence identities.

- bone, blood, fat, flesh and bat wing do not qualify merely because they are
  body-derived.

## Clue-strength caution

Organ is not a singleton tag, but its frequency is uneven by role in the ordinary
three-slot corpus.

A positive Organ fact can therefore be substantially stronger in some slots than
others.

Generator consequence:
- keep Organ visible as a true card property;
- score clue strength from actual candidate-field prevalence;
- do not assume every displayed property is an equally cheap ordinary clue.

This follows the already accepted separation between **display value** and
**deductive clue value**.

## Interpretation

Dark and Organ solve different parts of the design problem:

- **Dark** is the large global density/signature improvement.
- **Organ** is a smaller but coherent second layer:
  - world-grounded;
  - short label in English and Russian;
  - applies across Powder / Fluid / Essence;
  - creates useful late-card density;
  - reduces signature repetition;
  - increases semantic reasoning variety;
  - does not damage strong-surface availability or substance anti-repeat.

The four-property cards are not a failure condition under the newly accepted
product direction; they are natural candidates for richer later puzzles when
progression permits.

## Recommendation

**Organ passes the quantitative screen and is recommended for acceptance.**

Do not add a third broad tag merely to chase the old 25/50/25 soft histogram.
After Dark + Organ, reassess whether a further broad cross-cutting tag is
actually needed.

No installed-runtime test is required.
