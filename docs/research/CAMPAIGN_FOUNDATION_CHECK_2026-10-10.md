## Mixed knowledge and baseline preservation

`Investigation` now accepts `{edge: bool}` priors as well as legacy stable-edge
sequences. Choice logic reads earned facts only; negatives eliminate affected
hypotheses and cannot be bought again. Invalid non-boolean values fail closed.
`campaign_knowledge.py` stores provenance, rejects contradictory observations and
round-trips through JSON snapshots. Explicit experiments preserve both outcomes;
solved/known three-slot formulas establish both adjacent stable edges. Failed
mixtures record no inferred pair polarity; two-slot success establishes no
three-slot edge. This ledger is not yet connected to a browser profile.

The selector's existing earned-shortcut exemption also recognizes negative priors:
otherwise a legitimate short deduction could be penalized solely because its
knowledge was negative. Existing positive-only rows retain their old scoring;
weights and quality gates remain unchanged. Recomputed **57,600 original
route/held-out replays for 200 frozen packages** match every prior summary exactly.

Synthetic tests cover polarity, no unearned oracle reads, failed mixtures,
contradictions, snapshots, mutation detection, title independence, input reordering,
saved nested candidate semantics and certified-chain termination. A certified
compatible candidate need not experimentally refute every other unknown candidate.
